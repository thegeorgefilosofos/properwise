## LinkedIn

I'm in New York until the first of December, and I'd like to meet people who work in real estate, proptech, startups and venture capital.

I'm building PROPERWISE in Greece. It's an app for people who own property there: income, expenses, tenants, tax and deadlines in one place. You snap a bill and it files itself. Rental tax, form E2 and ENFIA (the annual property tax) are ready before your accountant asks.

The people who need this most are often not in Greece at all. Many Greek Americans own a family apartment in Athens or a house on an island, and they manage it from here through relatives, emails and an accountant they have never met. That's exactly the gap we're working on.

I'd especially like to meet:
- Greek Americans who own or invest in property in Greece
- investors and funds looking at Greek or European real estate
- proptech founders and operators who have taken a product across borders

If that's you, or you know someone I should talk to, send me a message. A coffee in Manhattan works, so does a call.
